Backups & privacy

Your meal logs, templates, workout sessions and progress measurements are kept in this browser's storage; your pictures are kept in local IndexedDB. No accounts or analytics are included. GitHub Pages serves this static app, but your logs are not uploaded to GitHub.

Restoring replaces your current tracker data. Backups containing photos may be large and sensitive. Store them privately; never commit backup files to your public GitHub repository.
